**Last updated:** October 11, 2026

ArrowNest uses Google AdMob to display rewarded and interstitial advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may collect and process information, such as device identifiers and advertising-related data, in accordance with their own policies and applicable consent requirements.

For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

ArrowNest is not specifically designed for children under the age required by applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us.

We do not operate our own server to store player account information as part of the current game functionality. Third-party services may process information as described in their policies.
