ArrowNest Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

About ArrowNest

ArrowNest is a mobile puzzle game. The game stores progress, settings, daily results, and statistics on the player's device as part of its current functionality. The developer does not operate a game account server for these features.

Advertising and Google AdMob

ArrowNest uses Google AdMob to display rewarded and interstitial advertisements. When ads are served, Google and its partners may collect or process information such as device identifiers, IP address, diagnostic information, and advertising interaction data, depending on the applicable settings, consent choices, and service configuration.

For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy and information about how Google uses data from apps that use its services:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

Local Game Data

Game progress, settings, daily results, and statistics are intended to remain on the device. This statement concerns the game's own storage and does not exclude data processing by advertising providers described above.

Consent and Advertising Choices

Where required, advertising and consent settings will be handled in accordance with applicable laws and Google AdMob requirements. Available choices may depend on the user's location and the version of the game.

Children's Privacy

The intended audience and age suitability of ArrowNest must be determined by the developer and accurately reflected in the game's store listing and advertising settings. If you have a privacy question or concern, please contact the developer using the details below.

Data Security and Retention

Game data stored locally may be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the game. Data processed by Google AdMob is subject to Google's applicable policies and settings.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the game's features, advertising configuration, or data practices change.
